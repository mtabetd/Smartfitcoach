2. Data collected

2.1 Identity data

First name, email address, date of birth, sex.

2.2 Health data (special category — Art. 9 RGPD / GDPR)

Warning: The following data is qualified as “sensitive data” by the RGPD (EU General Data Protection Regulation). Its collection requires your explicit consent, obtained upon your registration.
  • Biometric data: weight, height, waist circumference, estimated body composition, body photos (optional)
  • Medical conditions: diabetes (T1, T2, gestational), pre-diabetes, hypertension, eating disorders (ED), PCOS, menopause, chronic kidney disease (CKD), non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NASH), hypercholesterolemia, irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), dysthyroidism
  • Reproductive health: pregnancy status, week of pregnancy, pre-pregnancy weight, due date, menstrual cycle tracking (duration, date of last period), breastfeeding
  • Sports medical profile: injuries (shoulders, knees, back, etc.), functional limitations

2.3 Nutritional data

Food allergies, intolerances, diet (vegetarian, vegan, etc.), halal preferences, excluded foods, preferred cuisines, cooking level, daily food diary, hydration tracking, alcohol consumption.

2.4 Sports and performance data

Type of sport practiced, level, goals, training days, available equipment, loads used per exercise (1RM), session history (duration, calories, RPE, heart rate), progression.

2.5 Usage data

Interface preferences (dark mode, language), conversation history with the AI coach, gamification badges and streaks.

2.6 Data NOT collected

SmartFitCoach collects no geolocation data, places no cookies, uses no third-party analytics tools (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.), and performs no advertising tracking.

7. Data security

  • Encrypted connections in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Strict Content Security Policy (CSP)
  • Hashed passwords (bcrypt via Supabase Auth)
  • User data isolation (Row Level Security)
  • No personal data in application logs
  • Automatic cleaning of local data upon logout
  • Limited access to data on the principle of least privilege

8. Cookies and similar technologies

SmartFitCoach uses no cookies. Data is stored locally in the browser (localStorage) to allow offline operation. This data is automatically deleted upon logout.

9. Minors

SmartFitCoach is intended for persons aged 13 and older. For users under 16, the consent of a parent or legal guardian is required (Art. 8 RGPD / GDPR). Nutritional calculations incorporate specific protections for adolescents (caloric deficit capped at −300 kcal/day in accordance with ACSM 2007 recommendations).
